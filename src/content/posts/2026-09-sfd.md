## 引言 Introduction

我从 2025 年 11 月开始在 USTC Minecraft 维护一个基于 Fabric 的技术服务器；今年迎新开放的生存服务器，我在其中尝试了一个积分排行系统，一个跨服物品交换模组，以及一个外置登录和材质管理平台。在 2026 年，一个即将走向成年的游戏，依然能让一群人聚在一起，做一些有趣的事情，我想大概能去 SFD 分享一点东西吧。

Since 11/2025, I've been maintaining a Fabric-based technical Minecraft server at USTC. This year, with the release of the 2026 freshman survival server, I experienced a ranking system, a cross-over item-exchange mod, and a system for external login and texture management. In 2026, a game which is approaching its adult years, still gathers a group of people to do something interesting, and I think I can share some of these experiences at SFD.

Minecraft 卖了 4 亿份。但真正让它活了十七年的，是背后那个由自由软件支撑起来的生态。Notch 在 2009 年写它的时候以为只是个小项目，十多年后那些早期代码成了难以重写的地基。扩展性差、国内访问慢、启动器难用……官方没做好的，社区会给出更完善的答案。HMCL、Yggdrasil、Fabric……各种项目解决了启动、认证、扩展等核心问题。我做的一些事情——搭 Fabric 服、写开源模组、参与外置登录项目——在某种意义上，也是在重复这个逻辑：官方给不了的，我们自己造。

Minecraft has reached a total of 400M copies sold. But, what has truly kept it alive for 17 years is the ecosystem supported by free software. When Notch initially wrote it in 2009, he thought it was just a toy project. Over a decade later, those early codes have become a foundation that is difficult to rewrite. Shortcomings can be found in its extensibility, slow access in China, and a cumbersome launcher. What the official team neglected to address, the community has provided better solutions. Projects like HMCL, Yggdrasil, and Fabric, have solved several core issues such as launching, custom authentication, and extensibility. Some of the things I have done, such as setting up a Fabric server, writing open-source mods, and participating in external login projects, are, in a sense, repeating this logic - what the official team cannot provide, we create ourselves.

Minecraft 这个游戏的魅力，不在于它有多完美，而在于它有多大的留白。一个玩了十年的人，依然能在里面找到新的事情做。这些留白，让玩家、开发者、校园社团都有了动手的理由。借这个机会我想讲，这个游戏怎么让一群原本只是在玩游戏的人，开始搭服务器、搓代码、做开源项目、维护社区……

Charm of Minecraft does not lie in its perfection (actually, Minecraft itself is far from perfect), but in the vast amount of blank space it provides. Even after ten years of playing, one can still find new things to do within it. This blank space gives players, developers, and campus communities a reason to get hands-on. Taking this opportunity, I would like to talk about how this game has enabled a group of people, who originally were just playing the game, to start setting up servers, writing code, creating open-source projects, and maintaining communities...
